# Housing Market Lab: Cook County

Streamlit app: home price predictor plus microeconomics tabs (exogenous vs endogenous variables, consumer choice with
budget p_A·A + p_L·L, income and substitution effects, supply and demand, subsidy and tax incidence).

Data: Cook County Assessor sales 2013-2019 (real). Variables marked SIM (income, crime, schools, rent, days on market, etc.)
are simulated estimates for demonstration.

Files
- app.py: the website
- tool_models.json: trained models and town tables
- requirements.txt: packages
- train_tool_models.py: retrains the models from model_dataset.csv (optional)
